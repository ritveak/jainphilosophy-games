This laboratory is an exploration layer over two study notes. The **Canvas** follows the numbered hierarchy in *Karma Types and Subtypes* (KG-1). The **Gunasthan Matrix** looks up the currently transcribed *Bandh* and *Uday* entries from *Karma Bandh, Uday, Udirna and Satta based on Gunsthan* (KG-2).

The counts and classifications retain the conventions written in the notes. KG-1 records several counting conventions, including 158, 148, 120 and 122. Those counts describe different representations and are not combined here. Relationship records preserve source terms such as **Bandh Vichhed**, **Abandh**, **Uday Vichhed** and **Anuday**. “Not yet populated” describes the lab’s source coverage; it does not state a doctrinal absence.

This is a study aid based on the local notes, not a doctrinal verification. Embedded source images are not transcribed. Some KG-2 references point to umbrella concepts or non-KG-1 headings and remain flagged for mapping review.

Open the Laboratory tab to browse the hierarchy, search across collapsed branches, and inspect a Gunasthan/category combination. The source links in the inspector lead to the full hosted notes.
